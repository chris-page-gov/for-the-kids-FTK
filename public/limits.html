Evidence and limitations

A synthetic teaching artefact. This is a snapshot of one fictional early-years handover, not an operational service or an evaluated council integration.

What you can inspect

The demonstration includes 13 concepts and 17 directed relationships, with source references, an owned but unfinished action, a recorded handover receipt and a completed leaflet-dispatch event. The illustrated guide explains 22 steps with 32 actual screenshots.

What remains separate

  • A handover being received does not mean its next action is complete.
  • Sending information does not establish that support was received.
  • The family priority is an attributed practitioner summary in the fictional fixture, not a verbatim research quotation.
  • A matching identifier or a schema check does not establish a person's identity, lawful access or permission to share.
  • Bristol and Wigan are fictional locality variants. No real child records or working local adapters are included.
  • This standalone OKF 0.2 projection has not been tested as a Svelte Explorer import or complete Bundle Wiki profile.

Known demonstration limitations

  • The evidence check only tests that a completed action has evidence references and that their target records exist. It cannot test real-world truth.
  • The compact JSON omits the second locality fixture used by the on-screen comparison. An AI given only that JSON should not claim it has independently compared both fixtures.
  • The guide records a source-pointer limitation in the parent-card description and keyboard-focus issues in the original demonstration.
  • Relationship search is literal: “owner” and “owned” do not return the same results.
  • Authored reference answers are not live AI output. The recorded AI reading exercise was a Codex subagent trial, not an external chat-interface acceptance test.

Use it responsibly as a learning exercise

Keep these boundaries visible when presenting the pack. Use fictional records to adapt the workflow, and involve practitioners, families, information-governance specialists and service owners before considering real information or operational use.

The published files exclude authenticated collaboration material and third-party source packs. Release identity and hashes identify exactly which files were deployed.
